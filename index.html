JoyCrew Test

JoyCrew Test is a test application for a creator reward program. Creators sign in with their Google account and connect their YouTube channel. The app checks that videos registered by a creator belong to the creator's own channel and reads public video statistics (view counts, publish time, privacy status) to calculate rewards.

Google user data we access

  • Basic profile and email address (to identify the creator)
  • YouTube channel ID, title and thumbnail via the youtube.readonly scope (to verify channel ownership)
